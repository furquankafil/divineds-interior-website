Client Journey

A Considered Journey From Planning to Handover.

Understand the key stages involved in discussing, planning and executing an interior project.

Start at Step 01
01

Initial Consultation

Understand your goals, requirements, preferences and broad project scope — over call or WhatsApp.

02

Project Discussion & Site Assessment

Discuss the space and gather the information needed to evaluate what is possible.

03

Requirements & Space Planning

Identify priorities, functional needs and spatial considerations — storage, circulation, zones.

04

Design & Material Selection

Discuss suitable design directions, finishes and material choices for your approval.

05

Estimate & Client Approval

Present the proposed scope and applicable estimate for discussion and approval.

06

Execution & Installation

Coordinate agreed services — joinery, kitchens, ceilings, finishes and technical work.

07

Finishing & Quality Checks

Review visible finishing details and relevant work before handover.

08

Project Handover

Complete the agreed handover process for the approved scope.

We do not promise fixed delivery times, free design or site visits, 3D visualisations, unlimited revisions, warranties or cost guarantees. Anything offered will be stated in your written scope.
